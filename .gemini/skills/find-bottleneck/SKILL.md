# find-bottleneck

Keep asking where the human is the bottleneck, and answer it from data.

1. `wololo bottleneck` ranks signals from the board (blocked on human), reviews, queue, gate failures, rule hits and transcript interventions.
2. Take the top item and propose the environment change that removes it: a skill, a rule, a verification step, a source for `queue pull`, or a raised trust level.
3. File it as a task. Re-run later to confirm it dropped.
